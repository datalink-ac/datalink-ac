Datalink is designed for GitHub.

Read the story

Markdown is GitHub's first-class citizen, and a datalink is a picture made for it: the link serves SVG directly, the platform fetches it once and caches it — instant to open. Click a picture to enter /draw/explore: change the data, annotate, and share the new link.
